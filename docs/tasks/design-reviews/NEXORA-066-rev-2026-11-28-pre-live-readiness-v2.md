## 1. Purpose of revision v2 and delta over v1

Revision `v2` extends `rev-2026-11-28-pre-live-readiness-v1` (preserved unmodified on disk) to address all **PR #66 Tech Lead review findings (`REQUEST CHANGES`)**:

1. **Part A — Remediate Foundation CI instability (P1 Issues 1–3)** using root-cause fixes, sanitized failure diagnostics, strictly bounded transient discovery retries, and deterministic A/B regression tests (`Baseline A RED` -> `Candidate B GREEN`):
   - **Issue 1 (`scripts/dev-smoke.sh`)**: `compose up -d --no-build --force-recreate --wait --wait-timeout 180 api` omitted `--no-deps`, causing Compose (`compose.dev.yml`) to re-execute `builder`, `init`, `dependencies`, `migrate`, and `db-runtime` (rebuilding shared `dist` volumes while `sync-worker` `node --watch` remained running) and emit zero phase/health/listener/dependency diagnostics when `api` became `unhealthy` (`46110429506` attempt 1, job `140872509193`, 102s) or destabilized immediately after `Healthy` (attempt 3, job `140882948905`). Fix checks dependency readiness and recreates `api` with `--no-deps` when updating `ALLOWED_ORIGINS`, stops `sync-worker` alongside `api` & `web` around explicit `dependencies` rebuild, invokes bounded `probe_runtime` readiness, and adds sanitized `DEV_SMOKE_FAILED` JSON diagnostics via reusable `scripts/smoke-checks.mjs` helpers.
   - **Issue 2 (`scripts/browser-startup-diagnostics.mjs`, `scripts/public-https-browser.mjs`, `scripts/browser-login-smoke.mjs`, `scripts/pager-filter-browser-checks.mjs`)**: `readFile(join(profile, "DevToolsActivePort"), "utf8").split("\n")[0]` broke out of the poll loop immediately even on `""` or incomplete writes (`118ms` failure in `46110429506` attempt 2, job `140878205177`), ignored early Chromium termination in `public-https-browser.mjs` (`stdio: "ignore"`), and fetched `/json` without bounded timeout or page-target verification. Extract `waitForBrowserDevTools` into existing `scripts/browser-startup-diagnostics.mjs` (reusing `isProcessExited` and `browserStartupSummary`), enforce finite deadline polling for live child + integer port `1..65535` + bounded `/json/list` `page` WebSocket target, emit sanitized startup/phase diagnostics (`BROWSER_STARTUP_DIAGNOSTIC`) without raw stderr/secrets/URLs, and adopt it across all three CI browser smokes (`public-https-browser.mjs`, `browser-login-smoke.mjs`, `pager-filter-browser-checks.mjs`).
   - **Issue 3 (`scripts/dev-runtime-environment-probe.mjs`, `scripts/dev-smoke.sh:62`)**: Immediate one-shot `/proc` scan threw generic `DEV_ENV_ACTUAL_PROCESS_NOT_UNIQUE` during transient watch restart gaps right after container `Healthy` (`46110429506` attempt 3, `06:10:54.988Z` -> `06:10:55.291Z`), could throw raw `ENOENT` if a PID exited before reading `/environ`, did not re-verify PID/listener consistency post-`/environ`, and gave zero sanitized `candidateCount`/`listenerCount` telemetry. Split pure `/proc` snapshot verification (`verifyDevelopmentEnvironment`, injectable `fsOps`) from bounded transient wait (`waitForDevelopmentEnvironment`), re-verify PID `/cmdline` and socket ownership after `/environ`, retry ONLY transient lifecycle gaps (`NO_MATCHING_PROCESS`, `LISTENER_NOT_READY`, `PROCESS_EXITED_DURING_DISCOVERY`, `PROCESS_IDENTITY_CHANGED`, and transient watch handoff overlap) inside a bounded deadline, fail closed IMMEDIATELY (`attempts === 1`) on security/env mismatches (`DEV_ENV_PROPAGATION_MISMATCH`, `DEV_ENV_WORKER_IDENTITY_EXPOSURE`, `DEV_ENV_API_IDENTITY_MISSING`, `DEV_ENV_DATABASE_ROLE`, `DEV_ENV_PROBE_KIND`), and attach sanitized `DEV_ENV_PROBE_DIAGNOSTIC` JSON on persistent failure (never cmdlines, URLs, keys, or env values).

## 5. Alternative designs and trade-offs

- **Issue 1 Rejected Alternative**: Modifying `compose.dev.yml` (`depends_on` conditions) or retrying `--force-recreate` in a loop. **Rejected**: `compose.dev.yml` dependency wiring is correct for clean initial stack bring-up (`api` needs built artifacts first); masking a `--force-recreate` rebuild race with retries wastes runner CPU/time and risks hiding genuine startup regressions. Passing `--no-deps` on `dev-smoke.sh:82` after verifying prerequisites are healthy solves the root cause directly.
- **Issue 2 Rejected Alternative**: Increasing the 20s launch timeout or using Playwright/Puppeteer packages. **Rejected**: Increases attack-surface/dependencies (`KISS`) and does not fix empty-`DevToolsActivePort` `break` (`118ms` failure occurred on poll #1, not timeout).
- **Issue 3 Rejected Alternative**: Fixed `sleep` before `probe_runtime` in `dev-smoke.sh`. **Rejected**: Arbitrary sleeps are slow when ready and fragile under CPU load; bounded event/state polling on `/proc` + post-`/environ` snapshot re-verification is deterministic, fast when healthy, and fails cleanly at deadline.

## 6. Selected design and Tactical DDD alignment

No Tactical DDD domain model, Aggregate Root, Value Object, application use case, persistence port, database role, schema constraint, or API/SDK transport contract is altered. `Shipment` reconciliation invariants, `Carrier` purpose-separated crypto/vault custody, `Sync` fencing/pacing, `Identity` MFA/Origin/Host/Admin guards (`verifyPublicStartup`), and `UNISHIP_LIVE_CONTRACT_VERIFIED: boolean = false` remain strictly invariant (`ADR-008`, `ADR-009`). New helper code lives strictly under `scripts/` tooling with strict secret-sanitization and bounded-resource contracts.

## 7. Security, concurrency, and compatibility analysis

- **Secret privacy**: Sanitized diagnostics in `smoke-checks.mjs`, `browser-startup-diagnostics.mjs`, and `dev-runtime-environment-probe.mjs` emit ONLY fixed vocabulary codes, integers, booleans, and service names — never raw cmdlines, `DATABASE_URL`, ENV values, raw Chromium stderr, file contents, cookies, tokens, keys, or hostnames.
- **Bounded waiting & fail-closed**: Browser DevTools launch remains bounded (`<= 20s`); runtime probe wait remains bounded (`<= 15s`); permanent mismatches (`DEV_ENV_PROPAGATION_MISMATCH`, `DEV_ENV_WORKER_IDENTITY_EXPOSURE`, `DEV_ENV_API_IDENTITY_MISSING`, `DEV_ENV_DATABASE_ROLE`, `BROWSER_EXITED`, HTTP 4xx) terminate immediately on attempt 1.
- **Backward compatibility**: Synchronous `verifyDevelopmentEnvironment(kind, expected, opts)` signature stays valid; `waitForDevelopmentEnvironment` is additive; existing `createSmokeChecks` and `browserStartupSummary` signatures remain backward compatible.
